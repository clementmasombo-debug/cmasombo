About

Reporter at KTN News (Standard Group) since 2021, after five years as a videographer producing 500 features and documentaries.

I translate complex policy and development issues into evidence-based stories people can follow. My reporting documents under-reported communities, and I follow informed-consent, confidentiality and conflict-sensitive practices when covering sensitive subjects.

Since 2019 I have also worked as an independent media consultant and facilitator, developing communication content and training for journalists, civil-society organisations and community groups.

Alongside newsroom work I design and run media training on ethical communication, data privacy, human rights, and reporting on violent extremism for journalists, civil society and community groups.

Skills

  • Camera, sound, lighting and post-production
  • Content strategy, digital publishing and audience analytics
  • Strategic communications, media relations and stakeholder engagement
  • Training design and facilitation
  • Ethical, conflict-sensitive and consent-based reporting

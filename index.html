Featured story · KTN News

The Cry of Aberdares

A dispute over the proposed Mau Mau road, which would cut through Aberdare National Park, pitted the government and some residents against environmentalists. I reported the risks to the ecosystem, the alternative route opponents proposed, and why residents wanted the road: easier transport of farm produce to market and a link between Nyandarua and Nyeri counties. The Environment and Land Court later halted the project.
